NASA POWER root-zone soil wetness (GWETROOT, 0 = dry, 1 = saturated), monthly 1981-2025, downloaded 2026-11-14.
Each region = mean of a 4x4 grid of POWER points inside its box (points over open water dropped). Boxes as in src/lib/regions.ts.
Source: NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project (power.larc.nasa.gov), MERRA-2 based. Made by scripts/fetch-world-soil.mjs.
